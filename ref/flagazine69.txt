PREPARE THE STAFF

                                                 First, ensure that there are no large
                                                 splinters present on the staﬀ. If there are
                                                 any large splinters then remove them.



                                                 If there are any weak points in the staﬀ then
                                                 the staﬀ must be discarded.




Next, use the sandpaper to sand away any
smaller splinters and rough areas from the
staﬀ.




VERIFY THE STAFF

Verify that the staﬀ is smooth to the touch by
stroking it with medium grip strength.

ATTACH THE CLOTH TO THE STAFF

                                                                                     Lay out the staﬀ on the ground alongside
                                                                                     your cloth. The short side of the fabric
                                                                                     should be against the staﬀ.




                                                                                     On the staﬀ-side of the cloth, fold the fabric
                                                                                     back by approximately 1.5 inch and lay the
                                                                                     fabric over the staﬀ. The shorter side of the
                                                                                     folded fabric should be facing the staﬀ,
                                                                                     hiding the fold from onlookers.

                                                                                     Be sure to stretch the cloth taut along the
                                                                                     staﬀ




In the order of top, bottom, middle, hammer the cloth to the staﬀ using the tacks.
