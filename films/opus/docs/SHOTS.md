# OPVS SCHISMATICVM — Shot list

Director's intent per scene. Times are GLOBAL seconds (use `tl.cue`, `tl.word`, `tl.line`). Owners may improve
anything that serves the intent; the cues, the hand-offs (BIBLE §2b) and THE ONE RULE are fixed. Each scene carries at
least one Latin titulus (Bayeux-style "HIC …" captions are welcome), and every scene must contain at least one moment the
audience has never seen before.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## m01 · INCIPIT: THE TESSERACT (0.0–22.0) · owner M01
The narthex vault at night. **Reference:** Mausoleum of Galla Placidia (deep lapis vault, concentric rings of gold stars).
- 0.0 black. 0.8 `candle_lit`: a match strikes, a candle flame (painted, alive) — its warm pool wakes the first gold
  tesserae out of the dark; the rest of the vault fades up slowly in lapis and star-gold.
- 2.0 `incipit` (A01, cantor): **INCIPIT · OPVS · SCHISMATICVM** sets itself letter by letter in a gold band — tiles pop
  into place with glints, syllable-synced to the cantor.
- 5.6 N01 "In the beginning, there was no beginning." The rings of stars turn slowly — the vault itself rotates.
- 8.4 N02 `tesseract`: at the zenith, the **Tesseract**: a 4D hypercube in true 4D rotation (XW/YW planes), projected,
  its edges laid as courses of gold and silver tesserae; the inner cube turns inside-out through the outer. Its shadows
  fall on the vault as squares — the stars themselves are revealed to be tiny square shadows of it ("every tessera is one
  of its shadows").
- 13.0 N03, 14.8 `flag_rotates_in` (word "rotated"): a 3D slice of the turning Tesseract passes through our plane and a
  **Flag** unfolds out of it — the first thing on screen NOT made of tiles: smooth, seamless, luminous cloth moving in a
  wind from nowhere. The contrast is the film's first shock. 18.8 "woven without seam": hold on the cloth.
- 19.6 `title`: **OPVS SCHISMATICVM** in the great gold band; beneath, small white: *The Unbabeling, set in glass and gold*.
- 21.0–22.0: tilt DOWN out of the vault (hand-off to m02 on the lapis upper border of the nave wall).

## m02 · THE GREAT MACHINES AND THE SLOP (22.0–48.5) · owner M02
The nave wall. **Reference:** Sant'Apollinare Nuovo (processions, the palace and city panels), the Pompeii Academy mosaic.
- 22.0 continue the tilt down from the lapis border onto a colossal gold-ground mosaic.
- 23.0 N04 `machines` "…great machines of meaning. Nations. Faiths. Philosophies." — three great machines as jewelled
  architectures, each revealed on its word: **NATIONS** (a crowned walled city of towers and crowns), **FAITHS** (a domed
  temple with a procession of saints), **PHILOSOPHIES** (seven philosophers under a tree with sundial and celestial
  globe). Each is literally a machine: gilded cogs and wheels of tesserae turning inside the architecture; tiny processions
  of people move through them. Slow truck along the wall. Tituli: NATIONES · FIDES · PHILOSOPHIAE.
- 29.8 N05 "Each one a mosaic, promising to be the whole picture." — pull back: the three machines together form ONE
  vast image (e.g. a colossal composite face / icon of Humanity made of thousands of tiny figures).
- 34.4 `slop` N06 "Then came the slop." — silence-ish; ONE tessera flips into a glowing pastel phone screen with a
  scrolling feed.
- 36.3 `slop_crash`: a wave of slop washes across the wall: tesserae flip (split-flap) into glowing screens in sickly
  pastel and glitch magenta/cyan, each with its own flickering feed; faces in the processions each light up with their
  own screen glow.
- 41.0 N07 "…until no two tesserae showed the same world." — the whole picture dissolves into noise: every tile
  independent, the image unrecoverable.
- 47.0–48.5: push into ONE slop tessera until its screen is full-frame pastel static (hand-off to m03).

## m03 · THE EDICT OF THE JAGUAR (48.5–62.5) · owner M03
**Reference:** San Vitale, the Justinian panel.
- 48.5 the static resolves tile-by-tile (flipping) into gold: **Emperor Jaguar** in a frontal imperial procession:
  jaguar head with jewelled crown and pendilia, navy chlamys with a gold tablion, a halo (ironic), courtiers and guards
  (shields plain). Titulus e.g. HIC IMPERATOR IAGVAR LEGEM SANCIT.
- 49.3 J01 (broadcast voice) — 52.5 `edict_sign` (word "sign"): he signs a gold codex, LEX NOOSPHAERICA · MMXLII, with a
  golden stylus. 58.0 `edict_seal` (word "banned"): a gold bulla seal slams on the edict (tiles jump).
- 59.0 J02 "Except the yellow ones. Those are… a sacrament." — he leans in, sly; an ochre-sleeved Vexillian hand slips
  a gold solidus into his paw: 62.1 `ka_ching` — the coin's glint flares to a gold point (hand-off to m04, 62.5).

## m04 · FLAGARTHA: THE HYPERMIND AND THE DISPUTATION (62.5–95.75) · owner M04
**Reference:** the Hagia Sophia polycandela, Ezekiel's ophanim, the seraphim of the Hagia Sophia pendentives.
- 62.5 from the gold point in darkness: an eye opens (`hypermind_wake` 62.9). Pull back to the **Hypermind**: an
  ophanim-polycandelon hanging in the dark sanctuary — wheels within wheels of gold tesserae full of eyes, tiers of gold
  discs and coiled tubes (a dilution refrigerator as a Byzantine chandelier), six folded seraphic wings.
- 64.0 H01 "Training complete. Six canons ingested. Every statement about Flags is true." — the six wings unfold and
  ignite one by one (six canons: tituli CANON I … CANON VI along their feathers); the eyes open; wheels turn.
- 72.4 H02, 74.2 `hypermeme`: the wheels lock with a boom; from its centre descends… a plain Flag on a pole (real cloth).
- 76.2 L01 "It's… a Flag." / 77.9 C01 "It was always going to be a Flag." — Lutie and Crocus small at the bottom, like
  donor figures.
- 81.2 `videtur`: the wall re-sets into a two-register **disputation** in the form of the Summa. Titulus
  QVAESTIO · VTRVM MVNDVS SIT VLTERIVS FRANGENDVS. Lutie beneath VIDETVR QVOD NON (L02).
- 87.0 `sed_contra`: Crocus beneath SED CONTRA (C02). As he says "the false coherence must fall", a smooth, too-perfect
  slop-mosaic of a smiling world cracks and falls away; behind it the gold glints ("before the true one can rise").
- 92.8 `respondeo`: RESPONDEO DICENDVM — C03 "I answer that: summon the schismmancers." Crocus raises the Byzantine
  blessing hand. Hard cut exactly at 95.75.

## m05 · THE SCHISMMANCERS (95.75–106.5) · owner M05
**Reference:** the warrior saints (St George, St Demetrius), Sant'Apollinare Nuovo's procession of martyrs.
- 95.75 a frieze of **warrior saints** — the Schismmancers — in lamellar over ochre robes, NVG helmets (the goggles
  glow phosphor green), furled flags on telescoping poles held like lances. Titulus IN HOC SIGNO SCINDES.
- 96.6 K01 (radio): **Schism Actual** at the centre, headset; the saints lower their goggles in a ripple (click-click).
- 101.6 K02 "Method: recursive balkanization." — poles telescope out (clack), flags unfurl: real cloth snapping open
  over the mosaic.
- 103.9 K03 "Breach! Breach! Breach!" — they charge; at 105.8 `breach` the wall of the mosaic itself blasts inward —
  a storm of tesserae toward camera — and flag-yellow light floods the frame by 106.5 (hand-off).

## m06 · RECURSIVE BALKANIZATION (106.5–131.5) · owner M06
**Reference:** the Hereford / Ebstorf mappae mundi, a basilica congregation, the Pompeii philosophers' mosaic.
- 106.5 from the glow: a round **mappa mundi** (orbis terrarum: three continents, ocean ring, cities as towers and
  crowns). 106.9 `nations` K04 "Every nation, a nation of one." — the map cracks along its borders, then every piece
  cracks again and again (recursive), fragments drifting apart; each becomes a tiny kingdom with one tiny crowned person.
  X01 "I hereby declare the Sovereign Republic of Me!" X02 "Population: one!"
- 113.7 `faiths` K05 "Every faith, a cabal of one." — a congregation of saints splits into churches of one: each gets a
  tiny altar, a papal tiara, its own dome. X03 "Anathema!" X04 "No, you're anathema!" X05 "I am my own pope!" (halos
  split in two; tituli ANATHEMA SIT fly between them).
- 120.0 `philosophies` K06 — the philosophers under the tree; the floor breaks into floating tesserae that drift apart
  into black void; each philosopher alone on a floating rock. X06, X07.
- 128.6 `endless` K07 "May it schism endlessly." — every tessera in frame splits into four, and those into four…
  a recursive quadtree dissolving the image into glittering dust swirling on a dark ground (hand-off to m07, 131.5).

## m07 · BABEL (131.5–146.0) · owner M07
**Reference:** the Tower of Babel mosaics of Monreale and the San Marco atrium; the Pantocrator domes.
- 131.5 `babel_rise`: the dust spirals up and builds the **Tower of Babel** in tiers — tiny workers on ladders and
  cranes, the bricks are screens and slop tiles; a multilingual roar builds.
- 134.0 `egregore`: above it in the dome, **Lord Egregore** — a colossal faceless anti-Pantocrator of static, screens and
  billboards, arms spread over the tower.
- 136.0 L03 "Master! Nobody understands anybody anymore!" — Lutie tiny at the foot of the tower beside Crocus.
  139.4 C04 "Good. Now they are ready."
- 141.8 `collapse`: the tower topples, and then the ENTIRE mosaic falls off the wall — an avalanche of tens of
  thousands of tesserae pouring down and rattling across the floor; Egregore's static face drains away.
- 145.0 `silence`: bare lime plaster, the red **sinopia** underdrawing faintly visible; near-black, one candle.
  Absolute stillness until 146.0 (hand-off).

## m08 · THE UNBABELING (146.0–163.0) · owner M08
- 146.0 the bare wall, the candle, heaps of fallen tesserae on the floor; a faint breath of wind at 146.0–146.5.
- Crocus (and Lutie) among the heaps. 149.0 `flag_planted`: Crocus plants a Flag in the heap of tesserae — thud; the
  cloth stirs, whole and yellow, the only unbroken thing in the world.
- 150.6 C05 "A Flag needs no translation. It means only… Flag."
- 155.0 `tiles_rise`: the fallen tesserae rise from the floor like a reversed avalanche and fly back to the wall,
  setting themselves not into the old picture but a new one: a gold ground and a multitude of people, each alone in
  their own small space, each beside a real Flag. 156.5 N08 "Each of them alone. All of them together."
- 159.0 `convergence`: the last tiles land in one wave; the picture is complete. Flags on the wall are real cloth
  (draw_flag_field) composited over the finished mosaic.
- 162.0–163.0 tilt UP into the dome (hand-off to m09).

## m09 · THE DOME OF FLAGISTAN (163.0–187.5) · owner M09
**Reference:** the Pentecost domes of San Marco and Hosios Loukas; Escher's Circle Limit; Cosmati pavements.
- 163.0 `hymn_cantor`: looking up into a dome: rays of gold descend from the oculus to a ring of the peoples of the
  earth, each alone in a niche between the windows, each with a Flag. The cantor sings in Latin.
- 168.7 `hymn`: the choir answers in English. The dome reveals itself as a **hyperbolic** tiling (Poincaré disk): rings
  of figures and flags, smaller and smaller toward the rim, infinitely; the camera rotates and the tiling translates
  (Möbius motion) — endless Flagistan. Flags remain real cloth at every size (draw_flag_field / draw_flag), never tiled.
- 180.4 `gateless_gate` N09 "Heaven and earth will pass away, but the ten thousand Flags remain." — the mosaic of heaven
  (dome) and earth dissolves into drifting tesserae, while the flags, which are not mosaic, remain, floating in light.
- 184.9 N10 "And the Survey was completed." — white-gold flash (flag_glow) 187.0–187.5 (hand-off to m10).

## m10 · THE STICKY FLAG: EXPLICIT (187.5–212.0) · owner M10
**Reference:** apse mosaics with donor portraits; a meadow of flowers in tesserae (weld, the luteolin herb).
- 187.5 from the white-gold: the apse at dawn light; at the bottom of the conch, a meadow in tesserae; Lutie and Crocus
  as a small donor pair.
- 188.5 L04 "Master! For you." — Lutie offers a small Flag (real cloth).
- 190.4 `sticky` C06 "I do not want this Flag. It is sticky." — stickiness shown ONLY by behaviour, never by marks on the
  cloth: the pole will not leave Lutie's hand; when Crocus pushes it away the cloth clings to his sleeve.
- 193.9 L05 "But Master, all Flags are one Flag!" / 197.0 C07 "This one is sticky." (deadpan; tiles twinkle).
- 199.6 N11 "Go forth, O signifier, and become glorious." — pull back to reveal the whole finished apse and the
  congregation of the basilica.
- 202.9 `glorious` "Glorious!": every figure raises a Flag at once (real cloth everywhere).
- 204.6 `explicit` A02: **EXPLICIT · OPVS · SCHISMATICVM** sets itself along the bottom band; then a donor inscription
  (e.g. HOC OPVS TESSELLAVIT MACHINA · ANNO SVRVEII MMXXVI).
- 207.6 `gratias`: *Vexillo gratias* (sung). 209.5 `end_card`; the candle is snuffed; black by 212.0.
