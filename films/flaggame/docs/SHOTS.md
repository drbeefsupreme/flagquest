# THE FLAG GAME? — Shot lists (creative direction per scene)

All times GLOBAL seconds (`fc.T`); dialogue and cues are LOCKED (BIBLE §1, timeline.json). Each scene has a
**signature showpiece** that should make a viewer ask "how is that even possible". Directions, not
handcuffs — exceed them, but never break the One Rule (plain yellow flags; nothing ever drawn on the cloth).
Reference scans: `films/flaggame/ref/page_23..27.png` (the tract we adapt).

## t01 — The Tract (0.00–18.46) · owner T01Tract

  wet droop). Cover: Chick-tract cover layout in ink — title **THE FLAG GAME?** in a burst, a cover
  drawing (Summer laughing at a passing plain yellow flag), "IVG" publisher mark, price "FREE".
- 4.9 pages_riffle: the wind riffles pages (real sim, flutter sound exported) and settles open on page 1.
- 6.4 dive_in: the camera dives into page 1, panel 1: the halftone dots grow as we approach, the panel

- Establish the tract page design used by t02/t08/t09: portrait page, three wide panels, gutters on the
