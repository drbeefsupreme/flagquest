# THE UNBABELING — Shot lists (creative direction per scene)

All times are GLOBAL seconds (use `fc.T`). Dialogue times are locked (see BIBLE §1 / timeline.json).
Each scene has a **signature showpiece** — the thing that should make a viewer say "how is that even possible".
These are directions, not handcuffs: exceed them. Never violate the One Rule (plain yellow flags, no markings).

## s01 — Rotating Into Existence (0.0–21.0) · owner: Scene01
Signature: **a mathematically real 4D Crystal whose hyperplane cross-section becomes the first Flag.**
- 0.0–2.6 Pure black. Pinprick stars fade in, drifting golden dust motes.
- 2.6 (N01 "In the beginning, there was no beginning.") The Crystal condenses out of light: a 4D polytope
  (24-cell, tesseract, or 120-cell/600-cell subset) drawn as luminous white-gold edges with depth-cued
  width/alpha, rotated with true 4D rotation matrices (XW, YW, ZW planes) and perspective-projected 4D→3D→2D.
- 5.9 (N02 "…turning in a direction you cannot point to.") Rotation in a W-plane accelerates — the crystal
  turns inside-out through itself in a way no 3D object can. Faint radiating ley lines, slow camera orbit.
- 10.3 (N03) At the word "rotated" (`tl.word("N03","rotated")`) a glowing hyperplane slices the Crystal; the
  cross-section polygon morphs and settles into a **plain yellow rectangle**: the Flag's cloth. A pole draws
  itself as a line of light and becomes wood. 14.2 `flag_snap_open`: a gust — the cloth becomes a real
  cloth sim and SNAPS open (bloom flash). "A sign of itself": the Flag reflected endlessly in the Crystal's facets.
- 16.0 `title_slam`: IVG title card (full-frame yellow *page*, not a flag): thin white grid lines draw in with
  stagger; huge black Nimbus Sans Bold **THE UNBABELING** drops in letter by letter with overshoot; smaller
  "a Vexillomantic parable" / "Die Entbabelung — ein vexillomantisches Gleichnis"; tiny "IVG" top right,
  "revision 7" bottom left, "Expected release date: May 2577" bottom right (the Flagazine IVG cover).
- 20.3–21.0 the page slides UP out of frame, revealing flat `concrete_l` (hand-off to s02).

## s02 — The Age of Slop (21.0–47.5) · owner: Scene02
Signature: **a Hokusai tsunami made of thousands of AI-slop content cards, and a world splitting into feeds.**
- 21.0 Overcast `concrete_l` sky, a vast plain, fog. Crane up as three colossal *full-stack memeplexes* rise
  in parallax, each built like a tech stack of monumental layers:
  THE NATION — brutalist fortress-tower wrapped in border walls, crowned with a colossal crown, searchlights;
  THE FAITH — a gigantic domed temple beneath a floating halo ring;
  THE PHILOSOPHY — a colossal marble head/bust with a laurel, eyes closed, on a plinth of stacked books.
  (No flags, no real-world religious symbols.) Streams of tiny grey people march into their gates like ants.
- 22.0 (N04) each monolith lights up on its word: "Nations" / "Faiths" / "Philosophies" (`tl.word`).
- 28.4 (N05 "Full-stack memeplexes, each one promising to be the whole story.") broadcast rings pulse from
  antennas on each; the people inside move in perfect lockstep.
- 33.6 (N06 "Then came the slop.") a thin beige line rises on the horizon…
- 34.8–39.2 THE SLOP WAVE towers over everything: a curling wave whose body is a flowing particle field of content
  cards — uncanny grinning faces, six-fingered hands, "10 SHOCKING…" listicle blocks, thumbs-up icons,
  hearts, red notification badges with numbers, loading spinners, captcha grids, melting clocks — foam of
  bubbles and hearts; the curl is an infinite-scroll spiral. Glitch RGB split (use `post` ca), rising shake.
  39.2 `slop_crash`: it crashes over the memeplexes — they crack, topple, sink; beige flood.
- 40.6 (N07 "Unrelenting waves of it. A different feed for every face, until no two people lived in the same world.")
  Waist-deep in slop, isolated people each lit from below by a different screen glow (cyan, magenta, green, violet —
  never yellow). The frame tiles into split-screens of faces that subdivide recursively 1→4→16→64→256
  ("until no two people…") — a foreshadowing of balkanization.
- 47.0–47.5 push into one tile's screen → full-frame grey TV static (hand-off to s03).
